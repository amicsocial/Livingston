How the areas compare

Every location is measured with the same 5-mile circle. “Only in this circle” counts the households that the other two picks do not also reach.

How the three were chosen

Thirteen town centers and landmarks across the parish, from Denham Springs and Juban Crossing to Albany, Springfield and French Settlement, were each tested as the middle of a 5-mile circle, and every combination of three was scored on how many households they reach together, counting each household once. Denham Springs, Watson and Walker reach about 57,900. Port Vincent or Albany in place of Walker would add a little (about 2,100 and 100 households), but neither has office listings to match, and most of their extra households are outside the parish (see the table below).

With no limit on where the circles go, the best three circles centered anywhere in the parish reach about 84,000 households. None of those centers is in a town: one is on the parish line by the Amite River across from Baton Rouge, one is in the rural stretch between Denham Springs and Port Vincent, and one is off Clark White Road between Watson and Walker. All of the extra households are in East Baton Rouge and Ascension parishes; that set actually reaches fewer Livingston Parish households (about 29,800) than the three picks (about 33,400).

Denham Springs

The circle around Bass Pro Shops at S Range Ave and I-12 takes in Denham Springs, the Juban Rd area, the south side along LA 16 and, across the Amite River, the O’Neal Ln and Shenandoah areas of East Baton Rouge Parish. It holds about 35,000 households, about twice either other pick, though only 44% of them are in Livingston Parish. Three bail bond companies list a street address within 5 miles, about 11,670 households each. It is the farthest of the three from the jail, 12.6 miles in a straight line.

Watson

The circle around the Walmart Supercenter on LA 16 covers Watson, the Live Oak schools area, north Denham Springs and, across the Amite River, the eastern part of Central. It has about 18,800 households and the highest homeownership of the three (86%). Three bail bond companies list a street address within 5 miles. Two of them, both in north Denham Springs, sit near the edge of this circle and also of the Denham Springs and Walker circles.

Walker

The circle around Walker City Hall on Florida Blvd covers Walker, Juban Crossing and east Denham Springs. It has about 17,400 households, all of them in Livingston Parish. It overlaps the Denham Springs circle the most, so it adds the fewest new households (about 8,500). It has the most competition, 4 companies, including QRF Bail Bonds on Florida Blvd about 0.3 miles from the center, but it is also the closest pick to the jail at 6.5 miles.

Search demand and rankings today

Google does not publish how many people search for bail bonds in an area. Its only source of search counts is Keyword Planner in Google Ads, and Google has not allowed bail bond ads since July 2018, so it removes bail bond terms from those counts. On November 9, 2026, Keyword Planner returned “All keywords were removed” for “bail bonds” and “bail bondsman” in Denham Springs, and a keyword data service built on the same Google data returned zero. This page uses the two sources that do work: a Google Maps ranking grid for the current profile, and Google Trends.

Where the current profile ranks for “Bail Bonds”

A Google Maps ranking check run November 9, 2026 from 49 points, 5 miles apart, centered on the S Frost Rd office. Each dot is where the profile appeared in the map results for a search made from that spot. The full grid averages #4.57; points outside this map run east toward Hammond and south toward Lake Maurepas.

What the grid shows

The profile ranks #1 or #2 across the middle of the parish, around the S Frost Rd office, and slips the farther west a searcher is. Inside the Walker circle it already ranks #2 and #5, so an office there would mostly reinforce ground the profile already holds. The Denham Springs and Watson circles are where it ranks lowest inside the parish, #3 to #9 and #5 to #7, and the points toward Baton Rouge and Prairieville drop to #8 to #10. Those are the areas where a second address is most likely to lift the profile into the top three. The grid is one keyword on one day, so it should be rechecked after any new address goes live.

What Google Trends shows

Trends gives each place a score from 0 to 100 for the share of its searches that are about a term, not a count. Over the past 12 months Louisiana ranked second of all 50 states and Washington, D.C. for “bail bonds” (67, behind Texas at 100). Within Louisiana, the Baton Rouge metro, which includes Livingston Parish, scored 61, sixth of seven metros. In the Baton Rouge metro the most common bail bond searches were “bail bonds baton rouge” and “bail bonds near me”, and “bail bonds near me” grew 60% on the year, so searchers are leaning on location. Statewide, Walker, Louisiana was one of the fastest-rising topics searched alongside bail bonds (up 650%).

Trends does not report any single town in the Baton Rouge metro for bail bond searches, including Denham Springs, Walker and Watson, because the numbers are too small. Comparing the three circles on search demand therefore comes back to how many people live in each, which is the household count on page 2.

What to ask on each call

  1. Can the company name go on a building directory or tenant sign from the first month? No listing publishes this.
  2. Can the company name go on the office door itself, not just a monument or pylon sign? This is also never published.
  3. Will you rent to a bail bonds company? Some landlords and shared offices restrict this, so ask before anything else.
  4. Is the space available now, and will you do month-to-month with no minimum? Ask for the month-to-month price in writing.
  5. Is the office on the ground floor, with its own door from the parking lot or a short walk from the front entrance?
  6. Is someone on site during business hours, will mail addressed to the suite be accepted, and can someone verify the address by video or in person?

How this was put together

Business confirmed. 24/7 Livingston Parish Bail Bonds is a name (DBA) of Bust Out Bail Bonds LLC, phone (225) 330-2245, with an office at 29437 S Frost Rd, Suite 2, Livingston, LA 70754 and a second office in Amite under the name 24/7 Tangipahoa Parish Bail Bonds. Details are from the company’s own website. The current office was not treated as covering any area; it is shown on the maps for reference.

What the areas are ranked on. All households within 5 miles, since bail bond customers come from every income level. The fourth card figure is the straight-line distance to the Livingston Parish Detention Center, 28445 Charlie Watts Rd, Livingston, where Livingston Parish arrests are booked. The jail is placed at the middle of Charlie Watts Rd, next to the Sheriff’s Office, from OpenStreetMap.

Circle centers. Denham Springs: Bass Pro Shops, 175 Bass Pro Blvd, at S Range Ave and I-12. Watson: Walmart Supercenter, 34025 LA Hwy 16. Walker: Walker City Hall, 10136 Florida Blvd. All three were placed with OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim geocoder.

Choosing the areas. Thirteen town centers and landmarks in the parish (Denham Springs at Bass Pro, downtown Denham Springs, Lewis Vincent Elementary, the Range Ave Home Depot, Juban Crossing, Walker, Watson, Livingston, Albany, Springfield, Holden, Port Vincent and French Settlement) were each tested as the center of a 5-mile circle, and every combination of three was scored on all households reached, each counted once. For comparison, every point on a 0.1-mile grid inside the parish was also tested, and the best three circles were found by greedy search followed by swapping each center until no swap helped. That set reaches about 84,000 households, but with centers outside any town and fewer households inside the parish.

Households. U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 2020 to 2024 5-year estimates by census tract, for 243 tracts in Livingston and the nine parishes around it: households and renters (table B25003) and population (B01003). Each tract’s households are spread evenly over its land on a 0.1-mile grid, with lakes and rivers larger than about 0.3 square kilometers removed, and the grid cells inside each circle are counted. Tract estimates carry sampling error, so treat figures as plus or minus a few percent.

Competitors. Better Business Bureau listings in the Bail Bonds category, pulled for searches around Denham Springs, Walker, Watson, Livingston, Central, east Baton Rouge, Prairieville and Gonzales, read until the results were well past 25 miles from every center. A business counts if its name or main category is bail bonds and it lists a street address. PO boxes, listings with no address and a fugitive recovery business were left out, and 24/7 Livingston Parish Bail Bonds itself is not counted. BBB places many listings only at their ZIP code, so addresses near a circle edge were placed with Nominatim; those that could only be matched to a street or ZIP code are marked approximate. Google Maps listings were not used, and bail bond agents with no BBB listing are missing from every circle.

Rankings and search. The ranking grid is a 7 by 7 Google Maps rank check for “Bail Bonds” supplied as a PDF (Heatmap Audit Tool, November 9, 2026). Grid points were placed on the map from the town labels in that PDF; they are 5 miles apart and centered on the S Frost Rd office. Search scores are from Google Trends for “bail bonds”, past 12 months, checked November 9, 2026. Keyword Planner was checked the same day in an Amic Social Google Ads account.

Offices. Small offices and suites within about 5 to 6 miles of each center, found on listing sites (The Advocate real estate listings, LACDB, LoopNet, CommercialSearch, CommercialCafe, TenantBase, SmartMove, OfficeSpace.com, Showcase, Crexi, CoworkingCafe, LiquidSpace and Office Hub) and broker pages, checked November 9, 2026. None of the offices were visited or called. Ratings are green only when a listing says so. Office pins were placed with Nominatim where the street address matched; pins on LA Hwy 16, Juban Rd and Old South Walker Rd are approximate, placed from the listing’s own description of the location.

Map. Census tracts, interstates, highways and water from Census TIGERweb. Shading is all households per square mile of land, on a 0.3-mile grid. The color scale is set for a suburban and rural parish, so it is much lower than in big-city reports.
